Professional development clinic for conductors working with youth orchestras, focusing on age-appropriate repertoire selection, effective rehearsal techniques for young players, and building engaging educational programs.

Topics included communicating musical concepts to developing musicians, balancing technical instruction with musical expression, and creating inclusive ensemble environments that foster both skill development and artistic growth.
