Three-day residency including rehearsals, coachings, and lectures on orchestral conducting. Worked with the conservatory orchestra on standard repertoire while providing individual coaching sessions for conducting students.

Presented a public lecture on "Building a Conducting Career in the 21st Century," discussing audition preparation, networking, and balancing artistic vision with practical career development. The residency concluded with a concert performance conducted by both guest artist and selected students.
