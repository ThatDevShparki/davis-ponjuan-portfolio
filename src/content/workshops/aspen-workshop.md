Week-long workshop exploring specialized techniques for contemporary music, including extended notation, aleatoric elements, and collaboration with living composers. Participants prepared and conducted works by John Adams, Kaija Saariaho, Thomas Adès, and Caroline Shaw.

The workshop culminated in a public concert featuring world premieres of student composer works, conducted by workshop participants under mentorship.
