Teaching graduate-level orchestral conducting, score analysis, and rehearsal technique. Working with the next generation of conductors through private instruction, conducting laboratory sessions with student orchestras, and masterclass presentations.

Curriculum focuses on gesture economy, score preparation methodology, and the practical aspects of building a conducting career in the 21st century.
