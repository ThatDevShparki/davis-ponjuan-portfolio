As Music Director of the Metro City Symphony Orchestra, leading the artistic vision for one of the region''s premier cultural institutions. Responsible for programming eight subscription concert series annually, commissioning new works, and building educational initiatives that connect the orchestra with the community.

Under this tenure, the orchestra has expanded its reach through innovative programming that balances core repertoire with contemporary music, increasing audience diversity and engagement. Initiatives include a new music series featuring living composers, community partnership concerts in underserved neighborhoods, and expanded educational programming.
