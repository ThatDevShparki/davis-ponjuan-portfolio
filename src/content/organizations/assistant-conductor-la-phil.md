Served as Assistant Conductor to Gustavo Dudamel, preparing rehearsals for subscription concerts, conducting family and education concerts, and serving as cover conductor for all main stage performances.

Conducted over 25 concerts including educational programs, Hollywood Bowl summer series performances, and subscription concerts when called upon. Gained invaluable experience working with world-class musicians and soloists while deepening understanding of rehearsal technique and concert preparation at the highest level.
