Served as Associate Conductor for four seasons, conducting over 40 performances including subscription concerts, pops series, and educational programs. Assisted Music Director Peter Bay in rehearsals and concert preparation.

**Highlights**:

- Led annual Family Concert series (2019-2022)
- Conducted World Premiere of Sarah Kirkland Snider's _Something for the Dark_ (2020)
- Guest conducted Masterworks series substituting for Music Director (2021)
