Guest conducted three performances at the Barbican Centre in November 2023. Program featured Dvořák's Symphony No. 8 and Prokofiev's Piano Concerto No. 3 with soloist Yuja Wang.

The engagement marked my debut with the LSO and received enthusiastic reviews in _The Times_ and _The Guardian_.
