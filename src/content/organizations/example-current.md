As Music Director of the Metropolitan Chamber Orchestra since 2022, I have led the ensemble in reimagining the chamber orchestra experience for contemporary audiences. Our programming balances canonical masterworks with bold commissions from living composers.

**Key Achievements**:

- Launched the "Composers in Residence" program, commissioning five world premieres
- Expanded the orchestra's educational outreach to serve 15 Chicago public schools
- Introduced pre-concert talks and interactive program notes to enhance audience engagement
- Led the orchestra's first international tour to Germany and Austria (2023)

**Notable Performances**:

- Schubert Symphony No. 9 "Great" (Season Opening Gala, 2023)
- Stravinsky _Pulcinella_ with guest choreographer (Spring 2024)
- Complete Mendelssohn string symphonies cycle (Fall 2022)
