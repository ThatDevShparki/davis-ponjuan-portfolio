Awarded prestigious fellowship supporting early-career conductors through financial assistance and professional development programming. The fellowship included participation in the League''s national conference, mentorship from established music directors, and introductions to orchestra board members and administrators.

This fellowship directly led to guest conducting engagements with multiple regional orchestras.
