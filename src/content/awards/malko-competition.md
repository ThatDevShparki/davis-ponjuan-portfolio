Awarded second prize at one of the world''s most prestigious conducting competitions. The competition included three rounds with the Danish National Symphony Orchestra, culminating in a public concert at the Copenhagen Concert Hall.

The prize included engagements with orchestras across Scandinavia and mentorship opportunities with renowned conductors.
