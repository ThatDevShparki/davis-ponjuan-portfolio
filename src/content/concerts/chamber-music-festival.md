An intimate evening of Romantic piano quintets at Carnegie's Weill Recital Hall. Davis joined the Festival Chamber Players as pianist for this exploration of two masterworks of the chamber music repertoire.

The Brahms Quintet showcased the dramatic intensity and structural brilliance that makes it one of the cornerstones of the genre, while the Dvořák brought its characteristic warmth and Bohemian folk influences.
