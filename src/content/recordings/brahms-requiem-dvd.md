A complete video recording of the acclaimed Philadelphia Orchestra performance from September 2024. Features soloists Golda Schultz (soprano) and Matthias Goerne (baritone), along with the Westminster Symphonic Choir.

The Blu-ray includes bonus features with rehearsal footage and an interview discussing interpretative approaches to Brahms's masterwork.
