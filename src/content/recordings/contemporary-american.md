A celebration of contemporary American orchestral music featuring three landmark works from the 21st century. Recorded during guest appearances with the Los Angeles Philharmonic, this album showcases the vitality and diversity of American composition.

The recording received widespread critical acclaim and earned a Grammy nomination for Best Orchestral Performance, with reviewers praising the performances' technical precision and interpretative insight.
