Davis's debut solo piano recording explores two of Schumann's most personal and fantastical works. The Kreisleriana, inspired by E.T.A. Hoffmann's eccentric Kapellmeister Johannes Kreisler, showcases Schumann's dual nature through its contrasting Florestan and Eusebius moods.

The Davidsbündlertänze represents Schumann's imaginary league of artists fighting against musical philistinism, with each dance signed by either "F" or "E" to indicate its character.
