Ponjuan''s reading of the Alpine Symphony showcased both the work''s pictorial brilliance and its underlying architecture. The orchestra responded with playing of exceptional virtuosity, from the shimmering opening sunrise to the work''s majestic conclusion.

Particularly effective was Ponjuan''s pacing of the work''s long ascent and descent, maintaining dramatic momentum while allowing Strauss''s kaleidoscopic orchestration to register in all its detail. The performance confirmed this conductor''s mastery of large-scale symphonic writing.
