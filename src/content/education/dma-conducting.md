Focused on late-Romantic and early 20th-century repertoire with emphasis on the Austro-German symphonic tradition. Conducted over 40 performances with the Juilliard Orchestra and collaborated with world-class soloists in preparation for a professional conducting career.

Participated in masterclasses with Esa-Pekka Salonen, Marin Alsop, and Susanna Mälkki. Thesis work explored the structural and interpretative challenges of Mahler's symphonic writing, with particular focus on balancing architectural clarity with emotional depth.
