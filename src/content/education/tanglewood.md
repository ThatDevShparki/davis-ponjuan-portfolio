Selected as one of three conducting fellows for the prestigious summer program. Worked intensively with Andris Nelsons and the Boston Symphony Orchestra, conducting numerous rehearsals and public performances.

Studied contemporary music with composer-conductor Thomas Adès and participated in masterclasses with guest conductors including John Williams and Gustavo Dudamel. This transformative experience solidified approaches to orchestral balance, gesture economy, and score preparation that continue to shape artistic practice.
